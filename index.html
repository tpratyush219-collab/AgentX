What kinds of projects can we work on?

Landing pages, business websites, e-commerce interfaces, and custom web applications. We can also discuss design improvements or ongoing care for an existing website. The final scope depends on your goals and requirements.

Are the portfolio projects real client work?

No. Orbit and Forme are clearly labelled, self-initiated concept projects. They demonstrate a visual direction and approach; they are not commissioned projects, customer endorsements, or live products.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the size of the project, content readiness, integrations, and feedback. A realistic schedule and milestones should be agreed after discovery. No delivery window is promised before the requirements are understood.

How does AI fit into the process?

AI can assist with exploration, prototyping, and development, with human review guiding the final work. Advanced offerings such as autonomous agents, custom AI integrations, and workflow automation are coming soon and are not currently offered for purchase.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing is based on the agreed deliverables, complexity, integrations, and support needs. The packages above are ways to start the conversation. Actual fees, payment terms, third-party costs, and timelines belong in a written proposal.

Will the enquiry form send my message?

Not yet. Enquiry delivery is not connected. The form prepares a brief that you can copy or download to your own device. It does not send, upload, or save your information to a server. A verified contact channel will be added before launch.
